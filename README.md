# WealthNestPro – Home Loan & Property Loan Advisory Website

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-wealthnestpro.in-blue)](https://wealthnestpro.in)

WealthNestPro is a financial-services website focused on helping customers explore
and understand home loans, home loan balance transfers, and property-backed loan
solutions in India.

The website provides information about loan products, eligibility, documentation,
loan processes, and online financial calculators to help customers make informed
decisions before applying for a loan.

## Services

### 1. Home Loan

We assist customers in exploring financing options for purchasing or constructing
residential properties.

Typical use cases include:

- Purchase of a new home
- Purchase of a resale property
- Construction of a residential property
- Home loan eligibility assessment
- EMI estimation
- Documentation guidance

---

### 2. Home Loan Balance Transfer

Home Loan Balance Transfer helps existing borrowers explore the possibility of
transferring their outstanding home loan from one lender to another.

Customers can use our website to understand factors such as:

- Existing loan outstanding
- Current interest rate
- Remaining tenure
- Current EMI
- Proposed interest rate
- Potential interest savings
- Processing charges
- Other applicable charges

The final decision and loan terms are subject to the policies, eligibility
criteria, credit assessment, and approval of the respective lender.

---

### 3. Loan Against Property

Loan Against Property (LAP) is a secured financing facility where eligible
customers may use residential or commercial property as collateral, subject to
the lender's policies.

Potential use cases may include:

- Business requirements
- Education expenses
- Medical expenses
- Property-related requirements
- Debt consolidation
- Other permitted financial requirements

Loan amount, interest rate, tenure, eligibility and other terms depend on the
lender's assessment and applicable policies.

## Online Calculators

The website may provide financial calculators to help customers estimate loan
costs.

Available calculators can include:

### EMI Calculator

Calculate estimated monthly EMI based on:

- Loan amount
- Interest rate
- Loan tenure

### Loan Prepayment Calculator

Estimate the potential impact of making a partial or full prepayment toward

### Balance Transfer Calculator

Compare an existing home loan with a potential balance transfer scenario.

### Loan Against Property Calculator

Estimate EMI and repayment amounts for a potential Loan Against Property.

> Calculator results are estimates for informational purposes only and should
> not be considered a loan offer, approval, or financial advice.
